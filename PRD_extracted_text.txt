Outpatient Patient Scheduling & Care System
Product and Functional Requirements

Owner: ABC Health Care Company
Document type: Product Requirements Document (PRD) with Functional Requirements
Version: 1.0
Date: December 20, 2025

This document defines the product scope and functional requirements for ABC Health Care Company's
outpatient patient scheduling and care workflow system. It is designed to be implementation-ready and
compatible with integration to an existing EHR/EMR.

Intended audience: Product, Engineering, Clinic Operations, Clinical Leadership, IT/Security, Compliance

1. Product Overview

System name: Outpatient Patient Scheduling & Care System (OSCS)
Owner: ABC Health Care Company

Problem statement
Outpatient clinics often rely on fragmented scheduling tools and manual care coordination, leading to
no-shows, inefficient intake, inconsistent follow-up, and limited real-time visibility into capacity and patient
flow.

Goals (measurable)
- No-show rate: Reduce by 20-40% using confirmations, reminders, waitlists, and frictionless rescheduling.
- Scheduling effort: Reduce time to book an appointment by ~30% via eligibility rules and guided workflows.
- Access to care: Improve time-to-next-available appointment by 15-25% through capacity visibility and waitlist fill.
- Follow-through: Increase completion of follow-up tasks (labs, referrals, care plan items) by ~20%.
- Governance: Ensure audit-ready logs and role-based access for PHI handling.

Non-goals for MVP
- Inpatient bed management or hospital-wide operations.
- Full revenue cycle management (billing may integrate later).
- Replacing the EHR/EMR; OSCS should integrate with existing clinical systems.

2. Users and Roles

Primary personas
- Patient
- Front desk / Scheduling staff
- Clinician (MD/DO/NP/PA)
- Nurse / Care coordinator
- Clinic manager
- System admin / IT
- Billing staff (optional for MVP)

Access model
The system must implement role-based access control (RBAC) with least-privilege permissions. All
PHI-relevant actions must be audit logged for ABC Health Care Company governance and compliance.

3. Core Workflows

Scheduling (happy path)
- Patient requests an appointment (portal or call center).
- System finds eligible slots based on provider, location, visit type, and clinic rules.
- Appointment is booked and confirmation is sent.
- Patient completes pre-visit intake forms and consents.
- Day-of check-in (remote or front desk).
- Visit occurs; outcomes are documented.
- Follow-up tasks and care plan items are created and assigned.
- Post-visit communication and next appointment (if needed).

Exceptions
- No-show or late cancellation handling.
- Provider schedule changes requiring reassignment or rescheduling.
- Overbooking policies based on clinic rules.
- Waitlist slot offers when cancellations occur.
- Multi-appointment sequences (e.g., lab + consult).

5. Functional Requirements

A. Patient Portal and Access

FR-001 Patient registration and identity
- Patients can create accounts using email and/or phone.
- Support OTP verification; optional identity checks can be enabled by policy.
- Login attempts are rate-limited; authentication events are audited.

FR-002 Patient profile management
- Patients can manage demographics, contact info, preferred language, and communication preferences.
- Patients can add emergency contact information.

FR-003 Appointment request and self-scheduling
- Support instant booking for eligible visit types and a request-and-approve mode when required.
- Patients can search by visit type, location, provider preference, and time window.
- Only eligible time slots are displayed; double-booking is prevented unless allowed by policy.

FR-004 Reschedule and cancel
- Patients can reschedule/cancel within configured policy windows.
- Cancellation reason is captured (structured list plus optional free text).
- When a slot opens, the waitlist workflow is triggered (if enabled).

FR-005 Forms and documents
- Patients can complete intake forms, questionnaires, and consents digitally.
- Support e-signature for required consents.
- Signed documents are immutable and versioned; completion status is visible to staff.

B. Scheduling and Calendar Management

FR-101 Provider calendars
- Define working hours, schedule templates, breaks, and buffers.
- Template changes support effective dates to avoid breaking published schedules.

FR-102 Multi-location scheduling
- Support multiple clinic locations with distinct hours and resources.
- Support cross-location search for availability.

FR-103 Visit types and duration rules
- Visit types define default duration, required resources, prerequisites, and allowed booking channels.
- Visit types can be configured as staff-only or patient self-scheduling.

FR-104 Resource scheduling
- Appointments can reserve rooms and equipment where required.
- Conflicts are detected across provider, room, equipment, and staff resources.

FR-105 Waitlist and fill logic
- Patients can opt into a waitlist with preferred time windows and acceptable providers/locations.
- When a slot opens, offers are sent in priority order and expire after a configurable time.
- If not accepted, the slot is released back to availability.

FR-106 Overbooking rules
- Overbooking policies are configurable by provider, visit type, and time-of-day.
- When overbooking is used, staff must enter a justification; overbooked visits are visually flagged.
- All overbooking actions are audited.

FR-107 Scheduling constraints and eligibility
- Enforce constraints such as new vs established patient rules, age limits, insurance acceptance, and referral requirements.
- If constraints fail, the system blocks booking or routes to a request-and-review workflow.

FR-108 Appointment lifecycle states
- Appointment states include Requested, Scheduled, Confirmed, Checked-in, In-room, Completed, Cancelled, No-show.
- State transitions are validated and audited.

C. Intake, Check-in, and Clinic Flow

FR-201 Pre-visit intake
- Automated reminders are sent for incomplete intake items.
- Staff can view missing items per patient/appointment.

FR-202 Check-in modes
- Support front desk check-in and mobile self check-in (policy-gated by time and optional geo-fence).
- Optional capture of insurance card and ID images.
- Co-pay collection can be integrated (optional).

FR-203 Clinic status board
- Real-time board shows patient flow: arrived, waiting, roomed, clinician assigned, completed.
- Filters by provider, location, and visit type.

D. Care Management (Post-visit Follow-through)

FR-301 Care plan creation
- Clinicians and care team members can create care plans tied to the encounter.
- Care plans include goals, interventions, tasks, and due dates.

FR-302 Task management
- Tasks can be assigned to patients or staff; tasks include owner, due date, priority, and status.
- Overdue tasks escalate based on policy; reminders are sent as configured.

FR-303 Follow-up scheduling from care plan
- Generate follow-up appointment suggestions based on care plan tasks.
- Provide a 'schedule next' workflow from visit documentation.

FR-304 Care team messaging
- Internal messaging supports @mentions and templates (optional in MVP).
- Patient messaging is handled separately via the communication module.

E. Communication and Notifications

FR-401 Notification engine
- Support SMS and email notifications (MVP); optional IVR/push in later phases.
- Templates include confirmation, reminders (configurable timing), intake pending, cancellation/reschedule, waitlist offer, and post-visit instructions.
- Opt-in/opt-out preferences are stored and enforced; delivery status is tracked.

FR-402 Two-way messaging
- Patients can reply to SMS or message in the portal.
- Keyword routing supports automated actions (e.g., CANCEL) and staff inbox routing.
- Messages containing PHI are stored securely and audited.

F. Reporting and Analytics

FR-501 Operational dashboards
- No-show rate, late cancel rate, utilization, lead time, fill rate, and waitlist conversion.
- Staff productivity metrics (appointments booked, requests handled) where applicable.

FR-502 Clinical follow-through
- Task completion rates, follow-up adherence, referral completion.
- Clinic and provider-level views with role restrictions.

FR-503 Export and BI
- CSV export with role-based restrictions and audit logging.
- Read-only API access for BI tooling (optional).

G. Administration and Configuration

FR-601 Clinic setup
- Manage locations, hours, holidays, rooms, and equipment.
- Manage providers, roles, specialties, visit types, intake requirements, and policy rules.

FR-602 Templates
- Appointment templates by provider/location/visit type.
- Form templates with versioning and effective dates.

FR-603 Audit logs
- Track create/update/delete for appointments, patient demographics, care plans/tasks, and permissions.
- Audit entries include actor, timestamp, and before/after values; justification is captured when required.

H. Integrations

FR-701 EHR/EMR integration
- Support HL7 v2 and/or FHIR for patient and appointment data exchange.
- Define source-of-truth and conflict resolution strategy.

FR-702 Messaging provider integration
- Integrate with an SMS gateway and email provider; store message metadata and delivery receipts.

FR-703 Telehealth
- For tele-visit types, generate and attach a meeting link at booking (optional).

FR-704 Insurance eligibility
- Eligibility checks prior to confirmation (optional; depends on payer integration).

7. Non-functional Requirements

Security and privacy
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with least privilege and separation of duties where appropriate.
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (industry-standard encryption).
- Immutable audit logs for PHI-relevant actions.
- Optional MFA for staff; configurable session timeouts.
- Rate limiting and basic anomaly detection for authentication and portal usage.

Reliability and availability
- Target availability: 99.9% (configurable by ABC Health Care Company).
- Automated encrypted backups with defined RPO/RTO targets.
- Integration failures handled via retry queues and operational alerts.

Performance
- Availability search returns results in under 2 seconds for typical clinic data volumes.
- Clinic status board updates within 5 seconds under normal load.

Compliance and auditability
- Audit logs retained per ABC Health Care Company policy; exports restricted by role.
- Support patient access requests and record export processes per organizational requirements.

8. MVP Recommendation

MVP scope for ABC Health Care Company
- Staff scheduling with provider calendars, visit types, booking, and reschedule/cancel.
- Patient portal self-scheduling for limited visit types plus confirmations and reminders.
- Basic waitlist capability.
- Intake forms and check-in workflows.
- Basic care tasks and follow-up tracking.
- Operational dashboards (no-show and utilization) and audit logs.
- Baseline EHR integration for Patient and Appointment synchronization.

Phase 2 (post-MVP)
- Advanced resource constraints and capacity optimization.
- Two-way messaging with smart routing and ticketing integration.
- Insurance eligibility checks and payment/co-pay collection integrations.
- Telehealth integration expansion and deeper clinical documentation workflows.
- Advanced analytics and forecasting.

9. Definition of Done

- All appointment state transitions are validated and audit logged.
- Role permissions verified with a test matrix covering all personas.
- Security baseline tests (SAST and basic penetration testing) completed.
- Load testing validates scheduling search and status board performance targets.
- Integration failure handling validated (queue, retry, alert).
- UAT scripts for top workflows executed and signed off by ABC Health Care Company stakeholders.
