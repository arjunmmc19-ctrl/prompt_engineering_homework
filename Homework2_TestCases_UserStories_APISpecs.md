### Module 1: Patient Access and Portal

#### User Story 1: Patient Registration and Secure Login
* **As a** Patient,  
* **I want to** register and log in to the portal using my email or phone number verified with a one-time password (OTP),  
* **So that** I can securely access my scheduling portal and keep my clinical information protected.
* **Acceptance Criteria**:
  * **Given** a new user visits the registration page, **when** they enter their email or phone number, **then** the system sends a verification OTP to that address or number.
  * **Given** an existing user tries to log in, **when** they exceed the maximum configured invalid login attempts, **then** the system rate-limits the account and records an audited authentication failure.
* **Scope**: MVP
* **Related FR ID**: FR-001

#### User Story 2: Patient Profile and Demographics Management
* **As a** Patient,  
* **I want to** update my demographics, contact information, preferred language, communication preferences, and emergency contact details,  
* **So that** the clinic staff has my current information on file.
* **Acceptance Criteria**:
  * **Given** a patient is logged into their portal profile, **when** they edit their demographics, preferred language, or emergency contact information and click save, **then** the system stores the updated details.
* **Scope**: MVP
* **Related FR ID**: FR-002

#### User Story 3: Patient Appointment Self-Scheduling
* **As a** Patient,  
* **I want to** search for open slots and instantly book (or request approval for) an appointment by visit type, location, provider, and time window,  
* **So that** I can secure an appointment at my convenience.
* **Acceptance Criteria**:
  * **Given** a logged-in patient searching for availability, **when** they enter their visit type, preferred location, provider, and time window, **then** the system displays only eligible, open time slots in under 2 seconds.
  * **Given** the patient selects an open slot and submits the booking, **when** the request is processed, **then** the system prevents double-booking (unless allowed by clinic policy) and schedules the appointment.
* **Scope**: MVP (for limited, eligible visit types)
* **Related FR ID**: FR-003

#### User Story 4: Appointment Rescheduling and Cancellation
* **As a** Patient,  
* **I want to** cancel or reschedule my scheduled appointment within the allowed policy window and provide a cancellation reason,  
* **So that** the clinic knows I cannot make my slot and can offer it to someone else.
* **Acceptance Criteria**:
  * **Given** a patient has a scheduled appointment, **when** they attempt to cancel it within the allowed policy window, **then** the system prompts them to select a cancellation reason from a structured list and optionally enter free text.
  * **Given** a successful cancellation, **when** the appointment status changes to Cancelled, **then** the system automatically triggers the waitlist workflow for that open slot (if enabled).
* **Scope**: MVP
* **Related FR ID**: FR-004

#### User Story 5: Digital Intake Forms and Consent Signatures
* **As a** Patient,  
* **I want to** fill out intake forms, questionnaires, and e-sign consent documents digitally before my visit,  
* **So that** I do not have to fill out paperwork when I arrive at the clinic.
* **Acceptance Criteria**:
  * **Given** a patient has pending pre-visit forms, **when** they complete the forms and apply an e-signature to the consents, **then** the system saves the signed documents as immutable, versioned files and marks them complete.
* **Scope**: MVP
* **Related FR ID**: FR-005
